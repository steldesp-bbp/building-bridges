Eleni

When I first met Stelios I had absolutely no desire to start another long-term "therapeutic process". To survive the many "storms" that had passed over my soul since the age of 3, I had, with a passionate stubbornness to survive, taken my soul through a kind of amusement park of different psychotherapists with different methods, and I felt disheartened and angry. In all of them I had found a temporary emotional warmth, and all of them had worked to keep me alive and away from the asylum, but I had stopped believing in psychotherapists and in therapy as a release from the traumas of the past, which still held the reins of my emotional life. Until then I had always felt that in the work I did, I would get within a few metres of the top of the sand dune that separated me from the ocean of calm and quiet my soul was searching for, but I never managed to reach the top and see the sea. With Stelios I reached the top, went down the other side and have even taken a dip in the sea! I am not yet fully steady on my feet, so I still need our sessions, which are now only once every two weeks, but I am calmer than I think I have ever been. And for that I feel enormous gratitude.
